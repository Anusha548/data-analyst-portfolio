## Dataset

The practice dataset contains **6 rows and 9 columns**.

### Columns

* Order_ID
* Order_Date
* Customer
* Region
* Product
* Category
* Sales
* Profit
* Quantity

### Dimensions

* Customer
* Region
* Product
* Category

### Measures

* Sales
* Profit
* Quantity

### Grain

One order line or product per row represents the grain of the dataset.
